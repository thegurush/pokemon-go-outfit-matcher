Pokémon GO · Outfit Matcher

Offline-capable wardrobe reference · Universal matching rules v7 · No account or API key

1 · Pokémon palette

Upload a Pokémon image, then tap its body, claws, markings, and eyes to sample colors. Avoid the background. Tap a swatch to choose its role and weight.

Dominant colors should be sampled more than once; tiny accents (like eye yellow) should be sampled only once. This is an approximate color tool, not AI vision.

2 · Browse your actual wardrobe

Tap the center of an item on the screenshot to add it. The tool estimates its row and column; verify against the original page. Use the color score as a guide, not a final verdict.

3 · Your outfit candidates

Compare candidate items against the Pokémon palette. A strong match also depends on visible area, shaded tones, and small accents. Click an item to inspect or remove it.

4 · Saved outfits

Saved outfits stay in this browser's local storage. Export JSON for backup before changing phones or clearing browser data.

Inspired by the finalized Shiny Groudon outfit: yellow-olive armor, taupe torso, ivory claws, charcoal outlines, yellow eyes, and matching green backpack straps. An accessory is valuable when its visible parts improve the palette, even if its thumbnail looks different.

Static-site limitation: This first version provides interactive color sampling, wardrobe browsing, candidate scoring, and outfit saving. It does not yet automatically identify garment boundaries, distinguish fabric from screenshot backgrounds, or generate ChatGPT-quality recommendations. No image uploads leave your device.
